# Store Owner's Visual Management & Maintenance Guide

Welcome to your online store! You do **not** need any coding knowledge to run your store day-to-day. Everything is managed visually from your private admin dashboard.

---

## 1. Logging In to Your Private Admin Dashboard

1. Open your browser and go to: `https://yourdomain.com/store-control`
   *(Bookmark this link privately — it does not appear anywhere on your public website.)*
2. Enter your **Administrator Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign In**.
3. If you ever forget your password, click **Forgot or reset password?** on the login screen to receive a reset link by email.
4. Always click **Sign Out** in the bottom-left sidebar when using a shared computer.

---

## 2. Managing Products, Photos & Stock

Go to **Products & Stock** in the left sidebar.

### Adding a New Product
1. Click **+ Add New Product**.
2. Fill in **Product Name**, **SKU** (unique code like `KORA-HD-012`), **Category**, **Description**, **Fabric Details**, and **GSM** (e.g. `360 GSM`).
3. **Upload Photos**: Click **Upload Images** to select photos directly from your computer or phone gallery. The first photo becomes the main card image.
4. **Set Price & Discounts**:
   - Enter **Current Price** (the selling price).
   - To show a sale, enter the higher **Original Price** and **Discount %**.
   - Optionally set **Discount Starts** and **Discount Ends** dates for scheduled campaigns, or click **Clear Discount** to remove an expired sale.
5. **Set Size, Color & Stock Quantities**:
   - Under **Size, Color & Stock Matrix**, enter each Size (`M`, `L`, `XL`), Color Name, Color Swatch, and exact **Stock Quantity**.
   - Click **+ Add Variant** for additional size/color combinations.
6. Tick **Published (Visible)** and **In Stock**, plus **New Arrival**, **Best Seller**, or **Featured** as needed, then click **Save Product**.

### Quick Everyday Actions
- **Publish / Draft**: Click the `Published` / `Draft` button in the table to instantly show or hide a product.
- **In Stock / Out of Stock**: Click the stock badge to mark a product out of stock immediately.
- **Duplicate**: Click the copy icon to clone an existing product as a draft (great for adding new colorways or similar items fast).
- **Edit / Delete**: Use the pencil or trash icons on any row.

---

## 3. Managing Categories & Homepage Banners

- **Categories**: Open **Categories** to create new categories, upload category cover images, or change their display order (`1, 2, 3...`).
- **Homepage & Banners**: Open **Homepage & Banners** to upload new **Hero Carousel** photos, add mid-page **Promo Strips**, or update the **Top Announcement Bar** messages.

---

## 4. Managing WhatsApp Checkout, Delivery Charges & Store Settings

Open **Website Settings** in the left sidebar:
- **Brand Name, Tagline & Logo**: Upload your logo image or change your brand title.
- **WhatsApp Number**: Enter your business WhatsApp number with country code and no `+` sign (e.g., `88017XXXXXXXX`). All customer orders will open directly in this WhatsApp chat.
- **Delivery Charges**: Update **Inside Dhaka** (e.g., `70`) and **Outside Dhaka** (e.g., `130`) fees anytime.
- **Policies & Brand Story**: Edit your Delivery Info, Return Policy, Privacy Policy, Terms, and Brand Story text.
- Click **Save Settings** — changes appear live on the website immediately.

---

## 5. Tracking WhatsApp Orders

Open **WhatsApp Orders** to review orders submitted from the checkout form. Update each order's status (`pending_whatsapp` → `confirmed` → `dispatched` → `delivered` or `cancelled`) as you confirm them with customers on WhatsApp.

---

## 6. Routine Maintenance Checklist

- **Weekly**: Review **Out of Stock** count on the Overview dashboard and restock or unpublish sold-out items.
- **Monthly**: Clear expired promotional discounts and refresh Hero Carousel banners for new capsules.
- **Security**: Never share your `/store-control` password. If a team member leaves, suspend their account under **Admin Users** immediately.
